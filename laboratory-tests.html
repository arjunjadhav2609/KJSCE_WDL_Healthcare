CBC
A Complete Blood Count is a standard blood test that measures red blood cells, white blood cells, and platelets to evaluate overall health, diagnose conditions like anemia or infection, and monitor medical treatments more details
HbA1c
This test measures your average blood sugar levels over the past two to three months
LFT
A Liver Function Test is a group of blood tests that measures specific enzymes, proteins, and waste products to check how well your liver works. It helps find liver damage, infections, or diseases.
KFT
A Kidney Function Test, also called a renal function test, is a panel of blood and urine tests used to measure how well your kidneys filter waste, balance fluids, and manage essential minerals
ECG
A quick, safe, and painless medical test that records the electrical signals traveling through your Electrocardiogram - Mayo Clinic heart. It uses small sticky patches (electrodes) placed on your skin to map heart rhythm, rate, and structural changes.
